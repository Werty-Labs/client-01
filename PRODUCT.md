# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
International luxury travel audiences, honeymoons, and digital nomads seeking bespoke, tailor-made itineraries in Sri Lanka.

## Product Purpose
Tarragon Leisure provides expert, local, tailor-made travel planning for Sri Lanka, aiming to drive organic traffic and convert high-value clients through its blog and services.

## Brand Personality
Expert, bespoke, sophisticated, confident, and local.

## Anti-references
Generic OTAs (Online Travel Agencies), cluttered aggregators, "budget backpacker" aesthetics, AI-generated generic templates, and loud salesy marketing.

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell:** Use high-end visuals and editorial design to convey luxury.
- **Expert confidence:** Clear, direct answers and refined typography.
- **Haptic depth:** Interfaces that feel crafted, intentional, and premium.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for readability and support reduced motion preferences.
